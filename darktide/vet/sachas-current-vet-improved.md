## Selected Talents (30 Total)

### Core Path: Commando Branch to Infiltrate (11 Talents)

#### One Motion
- ×80% wield time (faster weapon swapping)

#### Out for Blood (requires One Motion)
- Gain 5% max toughness from all kills (10% from melee kills)

#### Toughness Boost (4-1) - MANDATORY SHARED NODE
- +15 base toughness (100 → 115)

#### Krak Grenade (from Toughness Boost) - YOUR BLITZ
- High single-target damage. Sticks to Flak, Carapace & Unyielding enemies. Base capacity: 2

#### Melee DMG Boost (6-3)
- +10% M.dmg

#### Agile Engagement (requires Melee DMG Boost)
- Melee kill → +25% R.dmg for 5s | Ranged kill → +25% M.dmg for 5s

#### Close and Kill
- You & AIC gain +5% movement speed

#### Crit Chance Boost (9-3)
- +5% T.crit chance (5% → 10%)

#### Infiltrate - YOUR COMBAT ABILITY
- Instant 100% max toughness + stealth for 8s with +25% movement speed. Staggers & suppresses enemies within 9m on leaving stealth. Base Cooldown: 30s

#### Hunter's Resolve (requires Infiltrate)
- ×50% TDR for 10s after leaving stealth

#### Duck and Dive (from Melee DMG Boost)
- Gain 30% max stamina from avoiding ranged attacks

## Build Summary

### Combat Ability
**Infiltrate** - Full toughness restore + stealth + ×50% TDR for 10s

### Blitz
**Krak Grenade** - High single-target damage, sticks to armored enemies

### Key Damage Synergies (KILLING MACHINE)
1. **Shock Trooper** - Lasgun crits consume NO ammo
2. **Exhilarating Takedown** - +15% toughness + ×72.9% TDR on ranged weakspot kills
3. **Precision Strikes** - +30% weakspot damage
4. **Killzone** - +20% ranged damage when no enemies within 8m
5. **Opening Salvo** - +10% crit chance at ≥80% ammo
6. **Agile Engagement** - +25% ranged damage after melee kill
7. **Rending Strikes** - +10% armor penetration
8. **Bring It Down!** - +20% damage vs Ogryns & bosses
9. **Fire Team** - +5% damage aura
10. **Longshot** - Up to +20% ranged damage at range
11. **Onslaught** - Up to 40% brittleness stacking

1. **Out for Blood** - 5% toughness on all kills (10% from melee)
2. **Confirmed Kill** - 10% toughness + 2%/sec for 10s on elite/specialist kills
3. **Exhilarating Takedown** - +15% toughness + ×72.9% TDR on ranged weakspot kills
4. **Infiltrate** - Full 100% toughness restore
5. **Hunter's Resolve** - ×50% TDR for 10s after leaving stealth
6. **Close Order Drill** - Up to ×67% TDR with allies
7. **Field Improvisation** - Corruption cleansing + toughness regen
8. **Duck and Dive** - +30% stamina when avoiding ranged attacks
9. **TDR Boost** - ×95% TDR
10. **2× Corruption Resistance** - Reduced corruption buildup
