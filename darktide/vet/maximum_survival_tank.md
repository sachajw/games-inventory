## PLAYSTYLE GUIDE

### Core Survival Loop

1. **Pre-positioning** - Use Camospec to spot threats before they see you
2. **Volley Fire Management** - Activate Volley Fire when:
   - Multiple elites incoming
   - Need the damage reduction window
   - Want to bolster allies' toughness
3. **Melee Emergency** - Power Sword special activation deletes anything nearby
4. **Block Everything** - With max block efficiency, you can block indefinitely

### Elite Priority Order

1. **Snipers** - One-shot threat, you have resistance but don't push it
2. **Mutants** - They grab you, preventing action
3. **Bombers** - AoE damage to you and team
4. **Crushers** - Heavy armor, high damage
5. **Other elites** - Trappers, Hounds, etc.

### Team Role

- **Anchor** - You don't die, teammates can rely on you
- **Spotter** - Pre-emptively identify threats
- **Toughness Battery** - Bolster grants team toughness

## AURIC-SPECIFIC TIPS

| Threat               | Counter                                      |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Bomber one-shots** | +20% Bomber resistance + 4 wounds            |
| **Sniper headshots** | +20% Sniper resistance + toughness stacking  |
| **Mutant grabs**     | +20% Mutant resistance + Power Sword special |
| **Tox Flamer DoT**   | +20% Tox Flamer resistance + high HP pool    |
| **Crusher rushes**   | Power Sword activation carapace penetration  |

### Toughness Management

- Let toughness break naturally - it regenerates fastest
- Don't block when unnecessary - save stamina for real threats
- Volley Fire activation gives temporary damage reduction
- Stand Your Ground + Knowledge is Power can stack significant DR

## BUILD VARIATIONS

### If you need MORE damage:

- Swap Helbore for **Boltgun** (IV Kraken)
- Change Ranged Perk 2 to: **2-5% Ranged Crit Chance**

### If you need MORE horde clear:

- Swap Power Sword for **Chainsword**
- Keep same perks, change Blessing to: **Blazing Spirits**

### If your team lacks support:

- Swap Keystone to **Bolster** variant
- Change Curio 3 Perk 2 to: **+4-10% Revive Speed (Ally)**
