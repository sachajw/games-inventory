# Boss Destroyer Veteran

## Ultimate Auric Build - "The Daemon Hunter"

**Build Philosophy:** Maximum single-target damage output to destroy Auric bosses as quickly as possible. Survival is secondary - kill them before they kill you.

---

## TALENTS

### Tier 1 (Level 5)

- **Destructive Rounds** - 20% increased weakspot damage
- **Doubles** - Free reload on kill (works on boss phases)

### Tier 2 (Level 10)

- **Priority Targeting** - 25% bonus damage to monsters (bosses)
- **Hunter's Respite** - Cooldown reduction on elite kills

### Tier 3 (Level 15)

- **Execution** - 30% bonus damage to targets below 50% HP
- **Fire Team** - Mark boss for team damage bonus

### Tier 4 (Level 20)

- **Volley Fire Sparker** - Chain Volley Fire to boss adds
- **Rapid Redeployment** - 25% reduced Volley Fire cooldown

### Tier 5 (Level 25)

- **Bring 'Em On!** - Up to 15% bonus damage based on nearby enemies
- **On Your Feet!** - Self-revive for mistakes

### Tier 6 (Level 30)

- **Volley Fire Mastery** - Increased damage, more shots, longer duration
- **Big Game Hunter** - Keystone - Massive bonus damage against monsters

## DAMAGE ROTATION

### Opening Rotation (Boss Spawn)

1. **Volley Fire immediately** - Mark boss + damage bonus
2. **Bolter headshots** - While Volley Fire is active
3. **Build Shred Profile** - 3-4 consecutive hits
4. **Maintain headshots** - Through Shred Profile bonus
5. **Reload during telegraphs** - Doubles talent saves ammo

### Mid-Fight Rotation

1. **Monitor Volley Fire cooldown** - Use as soon as available
2. **Track boss HP %** - Execution talent at 50%
3. **Shred Profile stacks** - Maintain 3+ for armor pen
4. **Crit awareness** - When you crit, spam shots
5. **Adds spawn** - Volley Fire Sparker for boss + adds

### Execute Phase (Below 50%)

1. **ALL Volley Fires** - No saving, all damage
2. **Bring 'Em On!** - Activate if adds present
3. **Execution talent** - +30% damage now active
4. **Melee if close** - Thunder Hammer charged
5. **Don't stop firing** - Race to kill before you die

### Emergency Melee Rotation

1. **Boss in your face** - Switch to Thunder Hammer
2. **Charged heavy** - Maximum damage
3. **Light interrupt** - If boss is winding up attack
4. **Back to Bolter** - Once space created

## BOSS WEAKSPOTS GUIDE

| Boss                | Weakspot             | Hitbox Size  |
| ------------------- | -------------------- | ------------ |
| **Chaos Spawn**     | Mouth (when open)    | Small        |
| **Daemon Prince**   | Head, joints         | Medium       |
| **Plague Ogryn**    | Back of head         | Medium       |
| **Beast of Nurgle** | Mouth                | Small/Medium |
| **Tormenter**       | Face, tentacle bases | Medium       |
| **Gorefiend**       | Head, chest          | Large        |

### Weakspot Hit Tips

- **Lead your shots** - Bosses move constantly
- **Wait for openings** - Some weakspots only open at certain times
- **Consistent hits** - Shred Profile rewards consecutive weakspot hits
- **Don't force** - Body shot is better than missing a weakspot

## PLAYSTYLE GUIDE

### Core Philosophy

> "The longer the fight goes, the more likely we are to die. My job is to end it as fast as possible."

### Positioning

- **Medium range** - Close enough for weakspots, far enough to react
- **Flanking position** - Side/rear weakspot access
- **Cover nearby** - For reloads, not for hiding
- **Escape route** - Boss charges = need to move

### Volley Fire Management

| Situation             | Action                        |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| **Boss spawns**       | Immediately activate          |
| **Sub-50% HP**        | Spam on cooldown              |
| **Add phase**         | Use to damage both            |
| **Boss telegraphing** | Don't waste, wait for opening |
| **Execute phase**     | Every single cooldown         |

### Ammo Management

- **Doubles talent** - Free reload on kills (save adds)
- **Time reloads** - Reload during boss telegraphs
- **Don't waste shots** - Every shot should be a weakspot attempt
- **Melee during reloads** - Thunder Hammer does damage while you're empty

## AURIC BOSS-SPECIFIC TIPS

### Auric Modifiers to Watch

| Modifier                 | Counter                                   |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| **Boss damage increase** | Kill faster, +1 Wound                     |
| **Boss enrage timer**    | Maximize DPS, save Volley Fire for enrage |
| **Increased spawns**     | Volley Fire Spacker helps                 |
| **Reduced healing**      | Don't get hit, kill faster                |

### Common Death Causes and Prevention

| Death Cause                                   | Prevention                               |
| --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| **Grabbed by Spawn**                          | Thunder Hammer special to break free     |
| **One-shotted in enrage**                     | Save +1 Wound for this                   |
| **No ammo** - spam fire                       | Time shots, use melee during reload      |
| **Stood in fire/pool** - not paying attention | Watch boss, not boss HP bar              |
| **Melee when shouldn't be**                   | Know your range, stay at Bolter distance |

## BUILD VARIATIONS

### Safe Boss Killer (Less Damage, More Survival):

- Swap Bring 'Em On! for **On Your Feet!**
- Add more defensive curios
- Longer fight, more likely to survive

### Maximum DPS (Glass Cannon):

- Drop ALL defensive perks for damage
- Swap Thunder Hammer for **Power Sword** (faster attacks)
- Kill boss in 30 seconds or die trying

### Support Boss Damage:

- Swap Fire Team for **Bolster**
- Keep team alive while they kill boss
- Less personal damage, more team utility
