## Universal Auric Survival Tips

### Core Survival Mechanics (All Builds)

1. **Toughness is your main HP** - Let it break, it regens fastest
2. **Don't get hit** - Positioning > armor
3. **Kill elites first** - They're the real threat in Auric
4. **Communicate** - Call out specials/elites
5. **Play to your build's strengths** - Don't force counter-play

### Priority Threat Order (Universal)

1. **Snipers** - One-shot potential
2. **Mutants** - Grab prevents all action
3. **Trappers** - Free kill for other enemies
4. **Bombers** - AoE team damage
5. **Flamers** - Zone denial
6. **Crushers/Maulers** - Heavy melee elites
7. **Hounds** - Disruptive
8. **Ragers** - Tunnel vision

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

- ❌ Chacking kills over survival
- ❌ Not blocking when you should
- ❌ Ignoring elite threats
- ❌ Playing alone/team separation
- ❌ Wounding Volley Fire on weak targets
- ❌ Not using melee when overwhelmed
- ❌ Poor ammo management

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Which build is best for solo Auric?

**A:** Maximum Survival Tank or Elite Hunter. Solo requires personal survival and damage output.

### Q: Which build is best for premade teams?

**A:** Squad Leader Support. Your team will appreciate the buffs.

### Q: Can I mix and match between builds?

**A:** Yes! These are templates. Adapt to your playstyle.

### Q: Do I need perfect gear rolls?

**A:** No, but they help. These builds work with adequate gear, excel with perfect gear.

### Q: Which build is easiest for beginners?

**A:** Maximum Survival Tank. Most forgiving, highest survival.

### Q: Which build has the highest skill ceiling?

**A:** Tactical Adjuster. Requires constant adaptation and decision-making.

### Q: Can I use these for lower difficulties?

**A:** Yes, they'll be overpowered. Good for learning mechanics.
