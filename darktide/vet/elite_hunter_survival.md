# Elite Hunter Survival Veteran
## Ultimate Auric Build - "The Big Game Hunter"

**Build Philosophy:** Kill elites before they can damage you. The best defense is a dead enemy. Maximum damage output against high-priority threats while maintaining solid survival stats.

---

## COMBO ROTATIONS

### The "Elite Delete" (Single Target)
1. **Spot with Volley Fire** - Marks the elite
2. **Double Bolter headshots** - Does 70-80% of their HP
3. **Thunder Hammer charged** - They explode, taking nearby enemies

### The "Oh Shit" (Multiple Elites)
1. **Volley Fire activation** - Damage bonus + mark all
2. **Bolter rapid fire** - Use Volley Fire bonus damage
3. **Thunder Hammer charged** - Explosion kills 2-3 at once
4. **Hammer light attacks** - Clean up wounded elites

### The "Ambush Response" (Surrounded)
1. **Thunder Hammer charged** - Delete nearest elite, explosion hits hordes
2. **Hammer light attack spam** - Stunlock and kill remaining
3. **Bolter when space created** - Pick off ranged threats

## PLAYSTYLE GUIDE

### Core Philosophy
> "A dead Crusher does 0 DPS. I am the Crusher's worst nightmare."

### Your Job in Auric
1. **Delete Snipers instantly** - They one-shot your teammates
2. **Remove Crushers** - They stop your team's advance
3. **Kill Mutants** - Before they grab your Psyker/Veteran
4. **Eliminate Flamers** - Before they zone your team
5. **Priority targeting** - You see them, they die

### Positioning
- **Medium range** - Close enough for accurate Bolter shots, far enough to react
- **High ground** - Better weakspot access
- **Near cover** - Not for you, for your reloads
- **Flanking position** - Catch elites when they focus on your team

### Volley Fire Usage
- **Single elite** - Don't waste it, Bolter is enough
- **2-3 elites** - Perfect Volley Fire target
- **Elite + horde** - Volley Fire + Bolter clear
- **Emergency** - Use for damage reduction window if needed

## WEAPON-SPECIFIC TIPS

### Bolter Mastery
- **Aim for heads** - 2x damage multiplier
- **Control fire** - Don't spam, every shot counts
- **Reload proactively** - Doubles talent gives free reload on kills
- **Lead your shots** - Bolter has travel time
- **Weakpoint knowledge** - Head/limbs for different enemies

### Thunder Hammer Mastery
- **Charge time** - Learn it perfectly
- **Release timing** - Don't overcharge
- **AoE awareness** - Explosion hits teammates (no friendly fire though)
- **Light interrupts** - Stop enemy attacks before they connect
- **Charged on Crushers** - Their armor means nothing

## AURIC DIFFICULTY TIPS

### What Kills You and How to Prevent It
| Threat | Counter |
|--------|---------|

| **Mutant grab** | +20% resistance, Hammer deletes them |
| **Ambush** | Volley Fire reveals all, Hammer AoE clears |
| **Crusher rush** | Charged Hammer = dead Crusher |
| **Rager tunnel** | Bolter headshots while they approach |

### When Your Build Fails and What To Do
| Situation | Adjustment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Too many elites** | Volley Fire + Bolter more aggressively |
| **Horde + elites** | Hammer charged hits both |
| **Ammo low** | Use Hammer more, save Bolter for elites |
| **Teammates dying** | Focus on what's killing them (usually Snipers) |

## BUILD VARIATIONS

### If you prefer MORE range:
- Swap Bolter for **Helbore Lasgun (IV)**
- Better accuracy at long range, faster fire rate
- Less burst damage, more consistent

### If you want MORE AoE:
- Swap Bolter for **Plasma Gun (IV)**
- Charged shots delete groups of elites
- Risk of overheating = higher skill ceiling

### If you need MORE utility:
- Swap Volley Fire Sparker for **Fire Team**
- Marked enemies take more damage from all sources
- Make your team deadlier too
