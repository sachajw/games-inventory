## THE MATH: WHY THIS WORKS

```
WITHOUT DR CURIOS:
Enemy hits you for 100 damage
You take: 100 damage
Die in 3 hits


WITH 3x DR CURIOS:
Each curio: ~25% DR
Combined: ~50% effective DR

Enemy hits you for 100 damage
You take: 50 damage
Die in 6 hits


WITH IRON WILL TOO:
When above 75% toughness: -50% damage

Enemy hits you for 100 damage
After curios (50% DR): 50 damage
After Iron Will (50%): 25 damage
Die in 12 hits


THAT'S 4x MORE SURVIVABILITY
From 3 hits to 12 hits
```
