# Squad Leader Support Veteran
## Ultimate Auric Build - "The Commander"

**Build Philosophy:** Make your entire team unkillable through buffs, debuffs, and team-focused talents. You are the force multiplier that turns a good team into a great one.

---

## TALENTS

### Tier 1 (Level 5)
- **Infiltrate** - Sprint efficiency for repositioning
- **Tactical Reload** - Faster reloads = more uptime

### Tier 2 (Level 10)
- **Camospec** - Spot elites through walls, info to team
- **Lingering Overwatch** - Extended Volley Fire duration

### Tier 3 (Level 15)
- **Fire Team** - Enemies marked by Volley Fire take increased damage from ALL sources
- **Mission Critical** - Bonus damage when reviving or assisting allies

### Tier 4 (Level 20)
- **Volley Fire Sparker** - Chain Volley Fire between nearby enemies
- **Bolster** - Activate Volley Fire to grant allies Toughness

### Tier 5 (Level 25)
- **On Your Feet!** - Increased revive speed, can self-revive once
- **Radiant Presence** - Allies within range gain damage resistance

### Tier 6 (Level 30)
- **Squad Leader** - Keystone - Your entire team gains bonuses based on your Volley Fire
- **Voice of Command** - Shout to grant temporary buffs to team

## ABILITY BREAKDOWN

### Volley Fire - "The Command Pulse"
**Primary Support Uses:**
1. **Bolster** - Grants all allies 20-30% toughness
2. **Fire Team** - Marked enemies take +20% damage from everyone
3. **Volley Fire Sparker** - Chain marks to multiple enemies
4. **Camospec** - You see all elites through walls

**Usage Protocol:**
| Situation | Volley Fire Use |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **Team toughness low** | Bolster them back up |
| **Elite wave incoming** | Mark all for +20% team damage |
| **Teammate downed** | Mark nearby enemies, then revive safely |
| **Horde + elites** | Mark elites, team focuses them |

## PLAYSTYLE GUIDE

### Core Philosophy
> "My job isn't to be the best killer. My job is to make sure no one dies."

### Positioning
- **Near mid-range team members** - Bolster range
- **Cover for reloads** - You're no good dead
- **Line of sight to threats** - Spot early, call early
- **Escape route planned** - In case things go wrong

### Toughness Management (Team Resource)
1. **Monitor ally toughness bars** - They're your responsibility
2. **Bolster proactively** - Don't wait for them to break
3. **Volley Fire cooldown** - Plan around it
4. **Multiple Bolsters** - In long fights, use it multiple times

### Revive Priority
| Priority | When | Why |
|----------|------|-----|
| **Critical** | Any ally in danger | They're about to permadeath |
| **High** | Ogryn/Zealot in melee | They're frontline |
| **Medium** | Other Veteran/Psyker | They have escapes |
| **Low** | Full health ally with full toughness | They don't need it |

## BUILD VARIATIONS

### More Offensive Support:
- Swap Recon Lasgun for **Boltgun**
- Keep same talents, delete elites faster
- Less spotting, more killing

### Pure Healer Support:
- Swap Revive Speed perks for **Toughness Grant** curios
- Focus entirely on keeping team alive
- Less personal damage, more team sustain

### Max Information:
- Add **Thermal Vision** talent
- See ALL enemies through walls
- Ultimate spotter, moderate damage

## FINAL NOTES

### You Are Successful When:
- ✓ No one dies (or dies rarely)
- ✓ Team always has toughness
- ✓ Elites are marked for bonus damage
- ✓ You communicate enemy positions
- ✓ You're the last one standing (because you kept everyone else alive)

### You Are Failing When:
- ✗ You die first (can't support from the spawn screen)
- ✗ Team has no toughness (Bolster mismanagement)
- ✗ You never mark enemies (wasting Fire Team)
- ✗ You don't call out threats (info advantage wasted)
- ✗ You chase kills instead of supporting (wrong mindset)
