# Melee Vanguard Veteran
## Ultimate Auric Build - "The Frontline Sharpshooter"

**Build Philosophy:** A melee-focused Veteran who thrives in the frontlines. Power Sword as primary weapon, ranged as backup. Maximum melee aggression with ranged precision when needed.

---

## TALENTS

### Tier 1 (Level 5)
- **Doubles** - Free reload on melee kill (melee sustain)
- **Blitz** - Increased melee damage for a short time after Volley Fire

### Tier 2 (Level 10)
- **Camospec** - Spot threats before you engage them
- **Counter-Attack** - Return damage when blocking attacks

### Tier 3 (Level 15)
- **Covered in Gore** - Regenerate toughness on elite/special kills
- **Execution** - Bonus damage to enemies below 50% health

### Tier 4 (Level 20)
- **Close Quarters Fighter** - Bonus melee damage when near enemies
- **Melee Expert** - Increased melee dodge range and stamina regen

### Tier 5 (Level 25)
- **Iron Will** - Temporary invulnerability when health drops below 25%
- **Man Down** - Increased damage when nearby allies are downed

### Tier 6 (Level 30)
- **Volley Fire Mastery** - Extended duration, more uses
- **Blitzkrieg** - Keystone - Melee kills reduce Volley Fire cooldown

## POWER SWORD MASTERY

### Special Activation Timing
| Situation | When to Activate |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Single Crusher** | On first hit - melts them |
| **Mauler/Rager** | On hit - kills in 2-3 swings |
| **Horde** | After 3-4 hits to build up |
| **Multiple elites** | Immediately - maximize damage |

### Attack Pattern
1. **Light attack spam** - Fast damage, builds up special
2. **Push attack** - Stagger, create opening
3. **Heavy attack** - High single damage
4. **Special activation** - Delete whatever is in front

### Block Management
With your block efficiency and stamina:
- **Block everything** - You have the stamina for it
- **Push to stagger** - Creates kill openings
- **Let toughness break** - It regens fast anyway
- **Special mid-block** - Activate without dropping guard

---

## COMBO ROUTINES

### The "Elite Blender" (Single Elite)
1. **Light attack x2** - Initial damage
2. **Special activation** - Massive damage boost
3. **Light attack spam** - They melt
4. **Push if needed** - Stagger and finish

### The "Horde Clearer" (Hordes)
1. **Light attack spam** - Fast horde damage
2. **Push attack** - Stagger multiple enemies
3. **Special activation** - AoE damage
4. **Spinning attacks** - If using Chainsword variant

### The "Ambush Response" (Surrounded)
1. **Push immediately** - Create space
2. **Special activation** - Delete nearest threat
3. **Light attack spam** - Kill weakened enemies
4. **Block remaining** - With max stamina, you're fine

### The "Elite Wave" (Multiple Elites)
1. **Volley Fire** - Mark all elites
2. **Special activation** - On biggest threat (Crusher)
3. **Light spam** - Chain through elites
4. **Kantrael** - Snipe remaining ranged specials

## PLAYSTYLE GUIDE

### Core Philosophy
> "My sword is my primary weapon. My gun is for when I can't reach them."

### Positioning
- **Frontline with Ogryn** - You can handle the melee
- **Flank position** - Catch elites from sides
- **Near Zealot** - Double melee pressure
- **Always pushing forward** - You're a vanguard

### When to Melee vs Ranged
| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| **Horde in face** | Power Sword, spam lights |
| **Elite in melee** | Power Sword, activate special |
| **Sniper far away** | Kantrael, headshot |
| **Flamer approaching** | Power Sword, close the gap |
| **Multiple elites** | Volley Fire mark, then melee |

### Toughness Management
- **Covered in Gore** - Elite kills return toughness
- **Confident Strike** - Hit chains return toughness
- **Let it break** - With 30% regen, it comes back fast
- **Block when low** - Preserve last bits for emergency

## AURIC MELEE SURVIVAL

### Threats That Kill Melee Players
| Threat | Counter |
|--------|---------|
| **Crusher overhead** | Dodge sideways, special during recovery |
| **Mutant grab** | +20% resistance + special activation |
| **Pox Hound pounce** | +20% resistance + block stun |
| **Flamer cone** | Close gap, special activation |
| **Trapper net** | If caught, special breaks free |

### When You're the Last One Standing
1. **Don't panic** - You're built for this
2. **Assess threats** - Priority: specials > elites > hordes
3. **Play patiently** - You don't need to rush
4. **Use your stamina** - Block everything
5. **Volley Fire + revive** - When area clear

## ADVANCED TECHNIQUES

### Special Activation Timing
- **Pre-activate on Crusher** - Before they wind up overhead
- **Mid-combo on Mauler** - After breaking their guard
- **Reactionary on Hound** - When they leap at you
- **Proactive on Rager** - They die before they can tunnel

### Block Cancelling
- **Attack > Block > Attack** - Faster attack reset
- **Push > Attack** - Instant stagger into damage
- **Special during block** - Maintain guard while dealing damage

### Spacing and Positioning
- **Always moving** - Don't get surrounded
- **Use terrain** - Funnel enemies through doorways
- **High ground** - Better for melee cleave
- **Near ammo** - You'll need it for Kantrael

## BUILD VARIATIONS

### Pure Aggression (Less Survival):
- Swap Block Efficiency for **Melee Damage**
- Change Iron Will for **Man Down**
- More damage, less margin for error

### Balanced Hybrid:
- Use **Chainsword** instead of Power Sword
- Better horde clear, worse single target
- Change Confident Strike for **Blazing Spirits**

### Tankier Melee:
- Add **Iron Will** AND **Covered in Gore**
- Swap Kantrael for **Boltgun** (more ranged burst)
- Less melee focus, more flexible
