# Tactical Adjuster Veteran
## Ultimate Auric Build - "The Adaptive Specialist"

**Build Philosophy:** Adapt to any situation through weapon swapping and flexible talents. You have the right tool for every threat - if you can identify it and switch to it in time.

## WEAPON SWAPPING GUIDE

### When to Use Each Weapon

| Situation | Primary Weapon | Why |
|-----------|---------------|-----|
| **Long-range Sniper** | Helbore Lasgun | Precision damage at range |
| **Close-range elite wave** | Bolter | Maximum burst damage |
| **Horde + specials** | Heavy Lasgun | Sustained damage output |
| **Ambushed in melee** | Power Sword | Best melee option |
| **Crusher rushing** | Power Sword | Special activation |
| **Multiple Snipers** | Helbore Lasgun | One-shot headshots |
| **Flamer approaching** | Power Sword | Close gap, delete |

### Weapon Swap Combos
1. **Helbore → Power Sword** - Snipe distant threats, switch for melee
2. **Bolter → Power Sword** - Burst damage elite, finish with melee
3. **Heavy Lasgun → Power Sword** - Clear horde, handle elites
4. **Power Sword → Helbore** - Create space, snipe specials

## ADAPTIVE TALENT USAGE

### Master Switcher Buffs
After swapping weapons, you gain:
- **First shot bonus damage** (Prepared Kill)

**Combo:**
1. Swap to Helbore
2. Headshot Sniper (bonus damage)
3. Swap to Power Sword
4. Special activation on Crusher

### Adaptive Tactics
Your damage type adjusts based on what you're fighting:
- **Armored enemies** = armor penetration bonus
- **Unarmored hordes** = cleave damage bonus
- **Specialists** = priority target damage bonus

## PLAYSTYLE GUIDE

### Core Philosophy
> "I have a tool for every problem. The challenge is identifying the problem quickly enough."

### Mental Checklist
Before every engagement:
1. **What am I fighting?** (Sniper, Flamer, Crusher, etc.)
2. **Where are they?** (Range, terrain)
3. **What's my current weapon?** (Is it the right one?)
4. **Should I swap?** (Bonus damage worth it?)
5. **Execute** (Kill efficiently)

### Positioning
- **Mid-range** - Access to both ranged and melee
- **Near cover** - For reloads and swaps
- **Line of sight** - To identify threats early
- **Escape route** - In case of misidentification

### When to Swap
- ** ALWAYS** - If current weapon is wrong for threat
- **Sometimes** - If swap bonus damage is worth it
- **Never** - If current weapon is adequate
- **Emergency** - Whatever saves your life

## ADVANCED TECHNIQUES

### The "Three-Weapon Rotation"
1. **Helbore** - Distant threats
2. **Swap to Bolter** - Close threats + bonus damage
3. **Swap to Power Sword** - Melee threats + bonus damage
4. **Repeat** - Always have bonus damage

### The "Pre-Swap"
- **Anticipate** - Know what's coming
- **Swap early** - Before seeing the threat
- **Always ready** - Right weapon for right time

### The "Quick Swap Cancel"
- **Attack → Swap → Attack** - Faster than reload
- **Melee → Swap → Shoot** - Instant ranged response
- **Shoot → Swap → Melee** - Instant melee response

## BUILD VARIATIONS

- Run **Heavy Lasgun** only (generalist)

- Less flexible, simpler to play

### Max Complexity (Constant Swapping):
