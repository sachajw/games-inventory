## Important: Psyker Mechanics

Before using these builds, understand Psyker's unique mechanics:

### Warp Charges
- Stack up to 6 charges
- Increase damage output
- Reduce peril generation (via Inner Tranquility)
- Some talents restore or consume them

### Peril
- Resource for psychic powers
- Builds up as you cast
- At 100%: you explode and die
- Can be "quenched" (dissipated) over time
- Venting Shriek removes 50% instantly

### Soulblaze
- Damage over time (DoT) effect
- Stacks additively
- Some talents interact with soulblaze kills
- Key component of several builds

## Universal Auric Survival Tips

### Core Survival Mechanics (All Builds)
1. **Toughness > Health** - Psyker has low HP, toughness is your main defense
2. **Manage your peril** - Don't explode, it ends runs
3. **Don't be the first target** - Positioning > armor
4. **Communicate** - Psyker has information advantages
5. **Play to your build** - Each has a specific role

### Priority Threat Order (Universal)
1. **Snipers** - One-shot potential
2. **Trappers** - Free kill for other enemies
3. **Mutants** - Only real counter to some Psyker CC
4. **Pox Hounds** - Disruptive
5. **Bombers** - AoE team damage
6. **Flamers** - Zone denial
7. **Crushers** - Heavy melee elites
8. **Ragers** - Tunnel vision

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- ❌ Exploding from peril (manage it!)
- ❌ Standing still while casting
- ❌ Not using melee enough
- ❌ Ignoring elite threats
- ❌ Playing alone/team separation
- ❌ Wasting Brain Burst (high peril, low damage without build)
- ❌ Forgetting Deflector blocks ranged too

## The 170 Toughness Threshold

This is THE survivability breakpoint for Auric difficulty.

**Why 170?**
- Prevents chip damage from basic melee attacks
- Basic attacks deal ~25 damage
- Below 170 toughness: you take chip damage to HP
- Above 170 toughness: full mitigation

**How to Reach 170+:**
```
Base Psyker toughness: 100
Kinetic Shield talent: +1
3x +20% Toughness curios: +60
Total: 161 (close, but not quite)

For perfect 170+:
- Need 3x +25% Toughness curios (rare)
- OR additional toughness from perks
- OR Warp Battery talent for +15% cap
```

## Psyker-Specific Tips

### Peril Management
- **Watch your bar** - Always know your peril level
- **Quell early** - Don't wait until 75%+
- **Use Think Fast** - If taking Venting Shriek build
- **Don't be greedy** - Better to quell than explode

### Positioning
- **Cover is your friend** - Charge staffs from safety
- **High ground** - Better angles for Brain Burst
- **Near team** - Psyker alone dies quickly
- **Escape routes** - Always have an exit

### Staff Usage
- **Lead your targets** - Projectiles have travel time
- **Don't overcharge** - Release at optimal damage
- **Learn breakpoints** - Know how many shots per enemy
- **Cancel charges** - Switch weapons to release instantly

### Melee Usage
- **You're not a melee class** - Use melee for survival/utility
- **Deflector blocks ranged** - Use it!
- **Confident Strike** - Returns toughness on hit chains
- **Special attacks** - Force sword special is incredible
