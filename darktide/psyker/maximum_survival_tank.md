# Maximum Survival Tank Psyker

## Ultimate Auric Build - "The Warp Fortress"

**Build Philosophy:** Transform the most fragile class into an unkillable frontline tank through Deflector mechanics, toughness stacking, and peril-based blocking. You become a mobile wall for your team.

## DEFLECTOR MECHANICS GUIDE

### How Deflector + Kinetic Deflection Works

**Normal Blocking:**

- Consumes stamina
- Blocks melee only
- Takes chip damage

**Deflector + Kinetic Deflection:**

- Consumes peril instead of stamina
- Blocks BOTH melee AND ranged
- No chip damage when blocking (full mitigation)
- -30% block cost from Deflector blessing

### Use Cases for Deflector Blocking

| Situation                     | Action                                           |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Reviving teammate in fire** | Block while reviving - absorbs all ranged damage |
| **Pushing gunline**           | Block and walk toward ranged enemies             |
| **Elite melee attacks**       | Block Crusher/Mauler swings with peril           |
| **Covering retreat**          | Team falls back, you block and cover             |
| **Horde + ranged**            | Block everything, let team shoot over you        |

### Peril Management While Blocking

**Block Cost:**

- Normal block: 1 stamina
- Deflector block: ~0.7 peril (with -30% blessing)
- Kinetic Deflection talent: Converts block cost to peril

**Peril Restoration:**

- **Murderous Tranquility** - Hitting 3+ enemies quells +5% peril per swing
- **Inner Tranquility** - -36% peril generation at 6 stacks
- **Ascending Blaze** - Warp charge on soulblaze kill
- **Venting Shriek** - Emergency peril dump + AoE stun

## PLAYSTYLE GUIDE

### Core Philosophy

> "I block what would kill my teammates. They shoot while I absorb. I am the wall."

### Positioning

- **Frontline with Ogryn/Zealot** - You can take the hits now
- **Between team and ranged enemies** - Deflector blocks for everyone
- **Near revives** - Block while getting teammates up
- **Choke points** - Doorways, halls where you can block everything

### Combat Rotation

#### The "Infinite Block" Tank Cycle

1. **Engage with melee** - Force Greatsword cleave
2. **Build soulblaze** - Blazing Spirit blessing grants toughness
3. **Block incoming damage** - Uses peril, not stamina
4. **Quell peril with hits** - Murderous Tranquility (-5% per swing)
5. **Repeat** - Near-infinite sustain

#### The "Revive in Fire" Technique

1. **Spot downed teammate in gunline**
2. **Activate Deflector** - Raise block
3. **Walk to teammate** - Absorbing all ranged damage
4. **Start revive** - Still blocking, still absorbing
5. **Complete revive** - Teammate lives, you took 0 damage

#### The "Elite Lockdown"

1. **Elite approaches** - Crusher, Mauler, etc.
2. **Activate special attack** - Infinite cleave wave
3. **Block their swing** - Uses peril, no stamina cost
4. **Counter-attack** - They're staggered, you cleave
5. **Victory** - Elite deleted, you took minimal damage

## WARP CHARGE MANAGEMENT

### Ideal Stack Count: 4-6

**Why 4-6:**

- **Inner Tranquility** gives -36% peril gen at 6 stacks
- Enough damage output
- Safe margin before peril gets dangerous
- Ascending Blaze procs more often

### Building Stacks

1. **Brain Burst** - 1 charge, manageable peril
2. **Staff secondary** - Varies by staff
3. **Kinetic Flayer procs** - Free Brain Bursts from staff attacks
4. **Ascending Blaze** - Soulblaze kills restore charges

### Maintaining Stacks

- **Don't let them drop** - You lose the -36% bonus
- **Use staff wisely** - Don't overspend peril
- **Melee between casts** - Quell with Murderous Tranquility
- **Watch your peril** - Don't explode

## BUILD VARIATIONS

### More Offensive (Less Survival):

- Swap a Gunner Resistance for **Combat Ability Regeneration**
- Change toughness blessing for **Health**
- More damage, less tanky

### Pure CC (Crowd Control):

- Use **Inferno Staff** as primary weapon
- Telekine Dome as blitz
- Less personal damage, more team utility

### Maximum Warp Charges:

- Swap to **Warp Siphon** keystone
- Add **Empyric Resolve** talent
- More casting, less direct combat
