## TRAUMA STAFF MASTERY

### Why Trauma Staff is Best

| Feature | Benefit |
|---------|---------|
| **Elite Stagger** | Locks down Crushers, Maulers, Ragers |
| **Special Stagger** | Stops Trappers, Hounds, Flamers in tracks |
| **Safe Damage** | Charge from cover/medium range |
| **Fast Charge** | Warp Flurry blessing chains quickly |
| **Low Peril** | Efficient compared to other staves |

### Target Priority with Trauma Staff

| Enemy | Shots to Kill | Stagger Effect |
|-------|---------------|----------------|
| **Sniper** | 1-2 charged | Complete stagger |
| **Scab Shotgunner** | 1 charged | Complete stagger |
| **Trapper** | 1-2 charged | Stagger prevents net |
| **Pox Hound** | 1-2 charged | Stagger prevents pounce |
| **Dreg Flamer** | 2-3 charged | Stagger stops cone |
| **Mauler** | 2-3 charged | Heavy stagger |
| **Reaper** | 3-4 charged | Heavy stagger |
| **Rager** | 3-4 charged | Stagger stops tunnel |
| **Crusher** | 4-5 charged | Interrupts overheads |

### Advanced Techniques

#### The "Basic Cancel" (Faster Staff Fire)
1. **Charge staff secondary** - Hold right-click
2. **Switch to melee** - Press 1 (releases blast, continues animation)
3. **Switch to staff** - Press 2 (ready to charge again)
4. **Repeat** - Significantly faster than normal fire rate

#### The "Advanced Cancel" (With Quell)
1. **Charge staff secondary**
2. **Switch to melee** (1)
3. **Short quell** (R) - Reduces peril buildup
4. **Switch to staff** (2)
5. **Repeat**

#### The "Sliding Cancel" (Mobility)
1. **Charge staff secondary**
2. **Switch to melee** (1)
3. **Slide** (Crouch + Sprint) - Invulnerability frames
4. **Switch to staff** (2)
5. **Repeat**

## COMBO ROTATIONS

### The "Elite Stagger Lock"
1. **Spot elite** - Crusher, Mauler, etc.
2. **Charge Trauma staff** - Full charge
3. **Release** - Heavy stagger
4. **While staggered** - Switch to Duelling Sword
5. **Finish with melee** - Elite dies before recovering

### The "Special Delete"
1. **Special appears** - Trapper, Hound, etc.
2. **Instant Trauma shot** - No need for full charge
3. **Switch to sword** - While they're staggered
4. **Confident Strike chains** - Return toughness
5. **Special dead** - Team safe

### The "Boss Cycle"
1. **Boss spawns**
2. **Trauma staff charged shots** - Stagger boss repeatedly
3. **Switch to sword** - When boss is vulnerable
4. **Weakspot hits** - Duelling sword excels here
5. **Back to staff** - Repeat cycle

## PLAYSTYLE GUIDE

### Core Philosophy
> "I don't kill elites - I stop them from moving, then finish them. Nothing gets close."

### Positioning
- **Medium range** - Trauma staff effective range
- **Cover nearby** - For charge time
- **High ground** - Better weakspot access
- **Flank position** - Catch elites from angles

### Staff vs Sword Decision

| Situation | Weapon |
|-----------|--------|
| **Elite at range** | Trauma staff stagger |
| **Elite in melee** | Duelling Sword |
| **Special appears** | Trauma staff (faster) |
| **Horde + elite** | Trauma staff both, sword finish |
| **Boss phase** | Staff stagger, sword damage |

### Movement & Kiting
- **Always be charging** - Staff charges while moving
- **Mind in Motion** - +20% speed after psychic powers
- **Slide cancels** - Invulnerability + mobility
- **Keep distance** - Trauma staff is ranged for a reason

## PERIL MANAGEMENT

### Peril Sources by Priority

| Source | Peril | Frequency |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| **Trauma staff secondary** | Medium | Primary damage |
| **Duelling Sword specials** | Low | Melee finishers |
| **Brain Burst** | Medium | Warp charge generation |

### Management Strategy

**Maintain 4-6 Warp Charges:**
- Inner Tranquility gives -36% peril gen at 6 stacks
- Enough damage output for Auric
- Safe buffer from explosion

**Quelling:**
- **Solidity talent** - +30% quell speed
- **Basic cancel** with melee - Natural quell during swap
- **Advanced cancel** with short quell - Active peril management

**When to Vent:**
- At 75%+ peril
- Between waves
- When no elites present
- NEVER during elite engagement (need staff ready)
