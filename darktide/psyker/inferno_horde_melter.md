# Inferno Horde Melter Psyker
## Ultimate Auric Build - "The Walking Apocalypse"

**Build Philosophy:** Lock down hordes with Inferno Staff primary attacks, melt everything with secondary soulblaze stacking. You are a walking inferno that consumes all in your path.

## INFERNO STAFF MASTERY

### Primary Attack: The Hard CC

**What it does:**
- Creates a cone of flames
- **Stuns everything hit** (except bosses and Mutants)
- Applies light soulblaze
- Low peril cost
- Fast fire rate

**Targets affected:**
| Enemy | Effect | Duration |
|-------|--------|----------|
| **Poxwalkers** | Stunned | ~2 seconds |
| **Dreg Crushers** | Stunned | ~1.5 seconds |
| **Scab Crushers** | Stunned | ~1.5 seconds |
| **Maulers** | Stunned | ~1 second |
| **Ragers** | Stunned | ~1 second |
| **Reapers** | Stunned | ~1 second |
| **Trappers** | Stunned | ~2 seconds |
| **Pox Hounds** | Stunned | ~2 seconds |
| **Dreg Flamers** | Stunned | ~2 seconds |
| **Gunners** | Stunned | ~1 second |
| **Snipers** | Stunned | ~1 second |
| **Mutants** | NOT affected | - |
| **Bosses** | NOT affected | - |

### Secondary Attack: The Soulblaze Engine

**What it does:**
- Charged projectile explosion
- Applies massive soulblaze stacks
- High direct damage
- Higher peril cost
- Chains with Warp Flurry blessing

**Soulblaze Mechanics:**
- Soulblaze = damage over time (DoT)
- Stacks additively (more stacks = more damage)
- Wildfire talent = +25% soulblaze damage
- In Fire Reborn = soulblaze kills restore warp charges
- Ascending Blaze = 10% chance for warp charge on soulblaze kill

## COMBO ROTATIONS

### The "Horde Lock" (Primary Attack)
1. **Horde approaches**
2. **Inferno primary spam** - Stun entire horde
3. **Everything stands still** - Team kills freely
4. **You continue stunning** - Infinite CC
5. **Horde dies** - Zero damage to team

### The "Soulblaze Melt" (Secondary Attack)
1. **Group of elites/horde**
2. **Charged secondary shot** - Into center of group
3. **Massive soulblaze applied** - Everything burning
4. **Switch to sword** - While they burn
5. **Clean up survivors** - They're almost dead from DoT

### The "Elite Wave"
1. **Multiple elites + horde**
2. **Inferno primary** - Locks down horde
3. **Inferno secondary** - Applies soulblaze to elites
4. **Sword finish** - While burning
5. **Warp charges restored** - Via In Fire Reborn

## PLAYSTYLE GUIDE

### Core Philosophy
> "I am the fire that consumes the horde. Everything burns, nothing approaches."

### Positioning
- **Frontline** - You control space with primary
- **Choke points** - Maximize primary cone coverage
- **Team between you and threats** - You're the first line of defense
- **Cover for charging** - Secondary needs charge time

### Staff Usage

| Situation | Attack | Why |
|-----------|--------|-----|
| **Horde approaching** | Primary spam | Locks everything down |
| **Elites in group** | Secondary into group | Soulblaze spreads |
| **Single elite** | Secondary + sword combo | Burst + DoT |
| **Boss fight** | Secondary charged shots | Soulblaze stacks |
| **Specials** | Primary to stun, sword to finish | CC then kill |

### Fire Pattern
1. **Lead hordes** - Primary before they reach you
2. **Center mass** - Hit as many as possible
3. **Follow up** - Secondary on elites/hordes
4. **Melee cleanup** - Sword while everything burns

### Threat Counters

| Threat | Counter |
|--------|---------|
| **Hordes** | Infinite primary stun, they never hit you |
| **Elite waves** | Primary CC + secondary soulblaze |
| **Gunners** | 20% resistance + Deflector sword |
| **Snipers** | 20% resistance + position carefully |
| **Mutants** | Only real threat - sword focus |
| **Trappers** | Primary stun prevents nets |
| **Pox Hounds** | Primary stun prevents pounce |
| **Tox Flamers** | 20% resistance + primary stun |

### Peril Management

**Why This Build Has Less Peril Issues:**
- Primary attack has low peril cost
- Inner Tranquility reduces cost further
- Battle Meditation quells on kills (lots of kills)
- In Fire Reborn restores warp charges
- Ascending Blaze also restores charges

**Peril Strategy:**
- **Primary spam freely** - Low peril, high effect
- **Secondary wisely** - Higher peril, use on groups
- **Melee during high peril** - Let Battle Meditation work
- **Quell safely** - Perilous Combustion adds explosion

## SOULBLAZE MECHANICS

### How Soulblaze Works

| Stack Count | Damage per Tick | Tick Rate | Duration |
|-------------|-----------------|-----------|----------|
| 1 stack | Base | ~1 second | ~4 seconds |
| 3 stacks | 3x Base | ~1 second | ~4 seconds |
| 5 stacks | 5x Base | ~1 second | ~4 seconds |
| 10 stacks | 10x Base | ~1 second | ~4 seconds |

**With Wildfire Talent (+25%):**
- All soulblaze damage increased by 25%
- Makes stacking even more valuable
- Single secondary shot can apply 5+ stacks

### In Fire Reborn + Ascending Blaze Synergy

**The Loop:**
1. **Apply soulblaze** - Via secondary attacks
2. **Enemies die from DoT** - Wildfire increases damage
3. **In Fire Reborn procs** - Restores warp charge
4. **Ascending Blaze procs** - 10% chance for more charges
5. **More warp charges** - More soulblaze applications
6. **Repeat** - Near-infinite casting
