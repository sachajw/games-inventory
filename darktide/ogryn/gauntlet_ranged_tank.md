## GAUNTLET MASTERY

### Why Gauntlet is Best Ranged

| Feature | Benefit |
|---------|---------|
| **High fire rate** - Sustained damage output |
| **Large magazine** - 100 rounds |
| **Good elite damage** - Carapace perk shreds Crushers |
| **Suppression** - Constant slow on enemies |
| **No accuracy penalty while moving** - Mobile turret |

### Fire Modes

**Primary Fire (Full Auto):**
- Holds 100 rounds
- Consistent suppression
- Elite deletion with crits
- Can fire while sprinting

**Secondary Fire (Braced):**
- Must stand still
- Perfect accuracy
- Better damage falloff
- Use for long-range Snipers

### Ammo Management

**With 100 round magazine:**
- Short bursts for accuracy
- Full auto for hordes
- Crits make every shot count
- Reload proactively

**Ammo Tips:**
- Headshots still matter
- Switch to Pounder when low
- Resupply for team too
- Don't waste on chaff

## SUPPRESSION SYNERGY

### Dual Suppression Strategy

**Gauntlet + Pounder:**
1. **Gauntlet suppresses** - Slows everything
2. **Pounder marks** - Weakspot vulnerability
3. **Team focuses** - Marked, slowed enemies
4. **Switch back to Gauntlet** - Repeat cycle
5. **Nothing approaches** - Perpetual control

### Suppression Value

**What Suppression Does:**
- Slows enemy movement
- Interrupts enemy attacks
- Reduces enemy accuracy
- Creates kill zones
- Protects teammates

**Best Suppression Targets:**
| Enemy | Suppression Effect |
|--------|-------------------|
| **Hordes** | Slowed to crawl |
| **Gunners** | Can't shoot back |
| **Snipers** | Interrupts aim |
| **Trappers** | Can't throw net |
| **Flamers** | Can't fire cone |
| **Elites** | Slowed approach |

## PLAYSTYLE GUIDE

### Core Philosophy
> "I am the turret that never stops. My gun is always firing, my enemies are always suppressed. I am the bullet hose of death."

### Positioning
- **Mid-range** - Gauntlet effective range
- **High ground** - Better angles
- **Near team** - They need suppression
- **Cover nearby** - For reloads

### Combat Rotation

#### The "Infinite Suppression"
1. **Spot group of enemies**
2. **Gauntlet primary fire** - Constant suppression
3. **Everything slowed** - Team picks them off
4. **Switch to Pounder** - When Gauntlet empty
5. **Pounder suppression** - During Gauntlet reload
6. **Back to Gauntlet** - Never stop suppressing

#### The "Elite Delete"
1. **Elite appears**
2. **Gauntlet focused fire** - Crits with Carapace perk
3. **Elite deleted** - Before reaching team
4. **Return to suppression** - Resume hordes

#### The "Braced Sniper"
1. **Sniper at long range**
2. **Gauntlet braced** - Perfect accuracy
3. **Sniper deleted** - Before they can shoot
4. **Return to mobile** - Unbrace and move
