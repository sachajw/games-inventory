## HEAVY EVISERATOR MASTERY

### Why Heavy Eviscerator is King

| Feature | Benefit |
|---------|---------|
| **Infinite cleave** - Hits unlimited enemies |
| **High bleed chance** - Applies bleed frequently |
| **Heavy attack special** - Revved saw for massive damage |
| **Good vs armor** - Carves through Crushers |
| **Fast light attacks** - Horde shredding |

### Attack Patterns

**Light Attack Combo:**
- Fast horizontal cleaves
- Hits everything in front
- Builds Wrath and Rage stacks
- Applies single bleed stacks

**Heavy Attack (Revved):**
- Hold to rev the saw
- Release for massive damage
- Applies 2 bleed with Hack and Slasher
- Infinite cleave through elites

**Special Attack:**
- Revved saw wave
- Maximum damage
- Best for Crushers and bosses

### Bleed Mechanics

**How Bleed Works:**
- Bleed = damage over time (DoT)
- Stacks additively
- Bloody Mess talent = +25% bleed damage
- Heavy Eviscerator applies bleed frequently
- Bull Rush + Knockout = 2 bleed on next melee

**Bleed Stack Strategy:**
1. **Bull Rush** into group
2. **Knockout procs** - Next melee applies 2 bleed
3. **Heavy Eviscerator light** - Hits 5-10 enemies
4. **10-20 bleed stacks applied** - Massive DoT
5. **They bleed out** - While you cleave more

## PLAYSTYLE GUIDE

### Core Philosophy
> "I don't just kill - I make them bleed. Every swing leaves enemies dying. The battlefield bleeds because I'm on it."

### Positioning
- **Frontline ALWAYS** - You're the melee king
- **Center of hordes** - Maximum cleave value
- **Between elites and team** - You tank with cleaves
- **Choke points** - Funnel enemies into your saw

### Combat Rotation

#### The "Bleed Explosion" (Elite Wave)
1. **Spot elite wave approaching**
2. **Bull Rush forward** - Knockout activates
3. **Light attack spam** - 2 bleed per hit
4. **Everything bleeding** - Switch to revved saw
5. **They die from bleed** - While you cleave more

#### The "Horde Blender"
1. **Horde incoming**
2. **Light attack spam** - Fast cleaves
3. **Wrath and Rage builds** - Attack speed increases
4. **Switch to revved saw** - When density increases
5. **Horde deleted** - Nothing remains

#### The "Boss Butcher"
1. **Boss spawns**
2. **Bull Rush** - Apply bleeds (Knockout)
3. **Light attacks** - Build attack speed
4. **Revved saw special** - Maximum damage
5. **Repeat** - Boss bleeds out fast

**How Bleed Keeps You Alive:**
1. **Apply bleed to elite**
2. **Elite focuses on you** - You have HP to tank
3. **Elite dies from bleed** - While you hit other targets
4. **Less incoming damage** - Dead elites deal none
5. **Team survives** - Because you killed everything
