## Important: Ogryn Mechanics

Before using these builds, understand Ogryn's unique advantages:

### Natural Stats
- **Highest HP in game** - 200 base (vs 150 for other classes)
- **Highest toughness** - Natural tankiness
- **Stamina bonuses** - More blocking capability
- **Large hitbox** - You ARE cover for teammates

### Unique Mechanics
- **Bull Rush (Blitz)** - Charge attack that knocks enemies back
- **Coherency radius** - Largest team buff range
- **Heavy weapons** - Access to exclusive heavy ranged weapons
- **Bleed specialization** - Some builds focus on bleed damage

## Universal Auric Survival Tips

### Core Survival Mechanics (All Builds)
1. **You ARE the tank** - Ogryn's job is to absorb damage
2. **Stay in front** - Between threats and team
3. **Use your HP** - 200+ HP means you can take hits
4. **Block when needed** - Especially with Pounder Shield
5. **Communicate** - Team relies on you for positioning

### Priority Threat Order (Universal)
1. **Trappers** - Nets prevent your tanking
2. **Mutants** - Grab interrupts everything
3. **Bombers** - AoE damages team
4. **Snipers** - One-shot teammates (not you)
5. **Gunners** - Suppression hurts team
6. **Crushers** - Heavy melee damage
7. **Flamers** - Zone denial
8. **Hordes** - Your specialty to handle

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- ❌ Playing from the back (you're not Veteran)
- ❌ Letting teammates die first
- ❌ Not using Bull Rush
- ❌ Ignoring Pounder Shield utility
- ❌ Wounding early (you have 4 lives, use them wisely)
- ❌ Being selfish with ammo boxes
- ❌ Forgetting you're the tank

## Ogryn-Specific Tips

### HP Management
- **200 HP base** - Highest in game
- **With 21% blessing** - ~242 HP
- **With 4 wounds** - Effectively 1000+ HP
- **Don't fear damage** - You can take it
- **Know when to back off** - Even Ogryns die eventually

### Positioning
- **Frontline ALWAYS** - You're the tank
- **Between team and threats** - Absorb damage
- **Choke points** - Hold the line
- **Cover for revives** - Block for teammates
- **High ground** - Better angles

### Ammo Management
- **Share ammo boxes** - Don't be selfish
- **Suppress wisely** - Don't waste rounds
- **Melee when appropriate** - Save ammo for elites
- **Kickback has lots of ammo** - Can be generous

### Team Communication
- **"Pushing up"** - Team follows your lead
- **"Suppressing [direction]"** - Team takes advantage
- **"Bull Rush to revive"** - Team knows you're going in
- **"Elite marked"** - After Pounder suppression

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Which Ogryn build is best for solo Auric?
**A:** Maximum Survival Tank or Bleed Cleave King. Solo requires survival and damage.

### Q: Which build is best for premade teams?
**A:** Any of them - Ogryn is always welcome. Gauntlet for coordinated teams, others for carry potential.

**A:** Yes! These are templates. Adapt to your playstyle.

**A:** No, but they help. Ogryn's natural stats carry you even with bad gear.
