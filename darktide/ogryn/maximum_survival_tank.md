**Build Philosophy:** Ogryn is naturally the tankiest class. This build maximizes that natural toughness with stacked HP, damage resistances, and the Pounder Shield for near-immortality.

## POUNDER SHIELD MASTERY

### How Pounder Shield Works

**Primary Fire (Suppression):**
- Suppresses enemies in a cone
- Applies Bullet Bracer debuff (weakspot vulnerability)
- Uses ammo
- Can be fired while moving (slower)

**Secondary Fire (Braced Mode):**
- Cannot move while braced
- Massive fire rate
- Accuracy improves over time
- Can't block while braced (unlike shield melee)

**Blocking:**
- Blocks both melee and ranged
- Consumes stamina
- Ogryn has high stamina naturally
- Block Efficiency perk reduces cost

### Pounder Tactics

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| **Horde incoming** | Suppress to slow them down |
| **Elite wave** | Suppress + mark for team |
| **Teammate pinned** - | Suppress enemies while reviving |
| **Choke point** | Brace and hold the line |
| **Crossing open area** - | Suppress for team safety |

## PLAYSTYLE GUIDE

### Core Philosophy
> "I am the wall. I am the shield. I am what stands between my team and death. Let them break themselves upon me."

### Positioning
- **Frontline ALWAYS** - You're the tank, act like it
- **Between team and threats** - Absorb damage meant for others
- **Choke points** - Maximize your blocking area
- **Near revives** - You can tank while reviving

### Combat Rotation

#### The "Meat Shield" Hold
1. **Position between team and enemies**
2. **Pounder suppress** - Slow everything down
3. **Block incoming damage** - When they get close
4. **Kickback finish** - What gets through suppression
5. **Never die** - You have 4 wounds and 300 HP

#### The "Unstoppable Rescue"
1. **Teammate downed**
2. **Bull Rush toward them** - Unstoppable activates
3. **Absorb all damage** - While immune to CC
4. **Reach teammate** - Start revive
5. **Block/Suppression** - Protect during revive

#### The "Elite Wave Response"
1. **Multiple elites spawn**
2. **Pounder suppression** - Mark them all
3. **Team focuses** - While you suppress
4. **Switch to melee** - When they get close
5. **Bull Rush escape** - If overwhelmed

## AURIC SURVIVAL GUIDE

### Threat Counters

| Threat | Counter |
|--------|---------|
| **Hordes** | Pounder suppression, they never reach you |
| **Gunners** | 20% resistance + 300 HP means nothing |
| **Snipers** | 20% resistance + high HP pool |
| **Crushers** | Block overheads, out-HP them |
| **Mutants** | 20% resistance + Bull Rush interrupt |
| **Bombers** | High HP means you survive bombs |
| **Trappers** | Only real threat - high HP helps survive |
| **Tox Flamers** | 20% resistance + HP regeneration |

### When You Take Damage (It Will Happen)

**HP Management:**
- **200-150 HP** - Normal operating range
- **150-100 HP** - Consider playing safer
- **100-50 HP** - Feel No Pain activates (30% ignore)
- **Below 50 HP** - Back off, let toughness regen

**Wound Usage:**
- **4 wounds total** - You can "die" 3 times
- **Don't waste them** - Each death is time lost
- **Strategic deaths** - Sometimes worth it to save team
- **Natural regen** - Ogryn heals wounds over time
